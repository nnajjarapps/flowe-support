Frequently asked

How do I book a session?

Open Discover, choose an instructor, tap Book, and pick a day and time. You'll see the request update in Bookings once the instructor responds.

How do payments work?

Flowe takes no payment for lessons — you settle directly with your instructor. Optional instructor Visible / Boost subscriptions are billed by Apple.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your data.

How do I report or block someone?

Use the ⋯ menu inside a message thread or on a profile.
