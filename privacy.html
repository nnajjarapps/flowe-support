Flowe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Flowe ("Flowe", "we", "us") connects Pilates students with independent instructors. This policy explains what information the Flowe iOS app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. We built Flowe to collect as little as possible.

How Flowe is built

Flowe has no separate company servers. Your data is stored in Apple iCloud (CloudKit) under your own Apple Account. Information you make public — such as an instructor profile or a community post — is stored in a shared area of iCloud so other Flowe users can see it. Private information stays in your personal iCloud storage.

Information we handle

  • Account & identity. When you use Sign in with Apple, we receive your name and an email address (which may be Apple's private relay). This identifies your account.
  • Profile information. If you list as an instructor, the details you add — display name, photo, bio, specialties, rates, and the approximate area you choose — are shown publicly so students can find you.
  • Bookings. Session requests you make or receive, including the chosen day, time and type.
  • Messages. Direct messages between users. Message contents are end-to-end encrypted — only you and the person you're messaging can read them.
  • Community content. Posts and photos you choose to share.
  • Location. If you tap "Use my location," it is used on your device to sort instructors near you and is not uploaded. Instructors choose an approximate area to display on their public profile — never a precise address.

How we use it

Only to make the app work: to sign you in, show instructor profiles, let you book and message, and display community content you choose to share. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we show no third-party ads.
  • We use no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Payments

Flowe takes no payment for lessons — you settle directly with your instructor. Optional instructor subscriptions (Visible / Boost) are purchased and billed by Apple; Apple handles that payment information under its own privacy policy. Manage subscriptions in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Sharing

Information you mark as public (your instructor profile, community posts) is visible to other Flowe users. We otherwise share personal information only with Apple (iCloud storage and in-app purchases) as needed to run the app, or where required by law.

Your choices & rights

  • Delete your account. In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This removes your data.
  • Block or report. Use the ⋯ menu on a profile or in a message thread.
  • Access or questions. Email us and we'll help — see below.

Children

Flowe is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; we'll revise the date above when we do.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email pilatesflowe@gmail.com and we'll reply within 1–2 business days.
